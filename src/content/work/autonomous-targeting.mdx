Department of War Directive 3000.09 requires "human judgment in the use of force," but does not define how or when a human must be involved — a gap that widens as AI-enabled targeting systems grow more capable. This memo, addressed to the Secretary of War in the voice of the Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Officer, weighs three paths: keep the current standard, tighten it into an enforceable human-in-the-loop requirement, or prohibit fully autonomous lethal systems outright. It recommends the second — a policy that resolves the current ambiguity, keeps the U.S. competitive against adversaries investing in autonomous systems, and positions Washington to shape international norms on responsible military AI rather than simply react to them.
