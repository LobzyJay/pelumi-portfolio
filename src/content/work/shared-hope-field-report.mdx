A spring internship at Shared Hope International, spanning policy research and analysis, legislative advocacy, and training and prevention programming. The recommendation drawn from that semester: start tracking digital-trafficking trends systematically, build partnerships with technology and research groups, fold digital-safety awareness into existing training, and position the organisation as a forward-looking voice in the policy conversation rather than a reactive one.

*"I want to contribute to ensuring that, as technology continues to evolve, it is used responsibly and in ways that protect vulnerable populations."*
